# Elliott147 — session-aware strategy decisions

14 November 2026. Integrates the invented146 session component with Elliott
context and setup decisions from section5 of the approved144 plan. This remains
research-only; it is not a qualified-price comparison runner or native adapter.

## Behavior

`Strategy` and `Policy` accept an optional coverage object. The new147 adapter
supplies an immutable, validated146 calendar. The default path keeps its existing
continuous-UTC coverage rules. Only `strategy.py`, `setups.py` and
`interpretations.py` change inside the strategy package; frozen117–126 source is
untouched.

In scheduled mode:

- Parent context checks the bars actually due under the declared calendar. A
  closure does not invent a missing daily/four-hour bar. The due check runs before
  cached context is reused; a missing parent blocks at its completion deadline.
- Count history and consecutive H1 setup bars can cross an explicitly declared
  closure. An omitted scheduled bar remains a coverage break. Historical price
  breaches remain permanent; a later rebound cannot revive an invalid count.
- Eligibility requires253 observed bars in every timeframe, every due scheduled
  bar in the declared prefix and an open session at the decision receipt time.
  All-timeframe coverage is checked even after the warmup count is reached.
- Existing formation/trigger separation, ten-H1-bar expiry, consumed setup
  identity, invalidation and competition rules remain. Expiry counts observed
  scheduled H1 bars and cannot refresh on a closure. A missing H1 bar still
  consumes the prior opportunity.

`DecisionReplay` provides a separate, transactional bar/clock journal. It accepts
the published invented146 bars in receipt order. Bounds, finalization times and
immutable lineage declarations are validated before updating strategy state.
There is no price file reader, quote interface, ledger, order submission or
transport in this driver.

One receipt's D1/H4/H1 publications can commit as an atomic batch. An invalid child
rolls back the entire batch, including an otherwise acceptable parent. Same-clock
publications must be unambiguous: one bar per timeframe in D1/H4/H1 order. Multiple
bars in one timeframe at the same availability are rejected by this profile.

A late bar whose source window precedes an already processed bar in that
timeframe rejects. The driver does not reorder/backfill prices into earlier
decisions. A late parent is permissible only if it remains appendable in its own
timeframe. Missing coverage blocks decisions while it is unavailable. This is a
deliberately conservative late-data profile, not a claim that the underlying146
observation component cannot represent such delayed data.

Checkpoints bind calendar, adapter/observation/strategy/control source hashes,
complete bar/clock prefix, counts, context, policy and immutable decision history.
Restore replays that prefix and compares the whole state. Tampering, altered
sources/calendars, duplicate journal entries and unknown event types reject.
The journal limit is enforced before mutation so accepted checkpoints stay
within the restore bound. These hashes provide local consistency, not provider
authentication or evidence qualification.

## Scope and next work

All16 market cells and native submissions remain unavailable. The driver records
decision signals only; it cannot establish an executed trade or observed profit.
Source/calendar/clock uncertainty, quote coverage, costs and prior-exposure
qualification remain unresolved. No source or acquisition dates are assigned and
no actual market data, account, terminal or native API is accessed in this sprint.
Protected dates and capture restrictions are unchanged.

The frozen controls retain their original UTC daily interface. They have not been
adapted to variable sessions, and unsupported schedules must stay unavailable in
their comparison path. The existing ledger's financing-clock differences remain.
Execution quote scheduling, pending-signal rechecks and funded session-aware
comparison are not integrated by this decision-only driver. Wider nested/fault
acceptance and disabled native demo reconciliation also remain unfinished.

The three strategy source changes intentionally change source bindings. Older
Engine/shared/study145 freezes and checkpoints must retain their original source
snapshots; they cannot be loaded across this change. Fresh invented replays need
a fresh binding. No prior artifacts are overwritten or silently migrated.
