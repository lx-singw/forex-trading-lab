acquisition or strategy experiment. The next requirement is an independent,
freely accessible EUR/USD bid/ask tick archive covering 2020–2023, with original
timestamp conventions, provenance and historical session evidence. A promising
source still needs a separately bounded acquisition and data-quality review.

the web tool. Search results guide discovery; only provider/publisher pages
support findings. Inspect GAIN Capital/FOREX.com, Darwinex, Pepperstone, OANDA
and other direct historical publishers as needed. Record actual use. No archive
GET/HEAD, quote payloads, API calls, downloads, accounts, authentication,
subscriptions, provider messages or identity changes. No stopped endpoint
retries: FXCM archives stopped after 403, Dukascopy after 429. Do not use their
mirrors as an access workaround. TrueFX login is already established and the

For each candidate distinguish documented facts from unknowns: exact year and
instrument inventory; tick versus candle resolution; separate bid and ask;
timestamp zone/DST/precision; historical trading sessions and feed gaps;
upstream identity/independence; access/account/payment conditions; use terms.

historical coverage. Search failure does not prove a source does not exist.

Preserve a dated result and machine-readable source notes, with direct URLs and
bounded quotations. No full copyrighted page copies. Record unavailable page
responses without retries and do not infer archive status from them. Hash the
plan before research and seal the resulting notes/report; preserve all previous
frozen code, plans, reports and datasets. Update editable handoff documents.

If an accessible candidate meets the documentation requirements, propose a
separate bounded metadata/acquisition plan. Otherwise document the specific
missing evidence and choose the next useful demo-only task. Do not lower data

activate orders. Historical costs and profitability remain unverified. Do not
use consumed 2025 or reserved November–December 2026 data. Pure documentation
