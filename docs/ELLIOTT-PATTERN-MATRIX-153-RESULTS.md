# Elliott153 — candle-derived nested catalogue matrix

15 November 2026. Adds reproducible, constructed H1 candles that exercise the
confirmed-pivot detector and default bounded parser for every finite catalogue
family in both directions, plus single and multiple third-level impulse extensions.
Trading/runtime, risk, cost, previous generator and frozen-control sources remain
unchanged. This advances the144 plan's parser acceptance; it does not establish
complete family coverage or market performance.

## Fixed matrix and input scope

The CLI declares46 cases and source hashes before inspecting any result:

| Group | Cases | Result in this corpus |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 18 catalogue families, bullish and bearish | 36 | Exact nested topology recognized |
| Impulse extensions at1,3,5,1+3 and1+3+5, both directions | 10 | Exact third-level topology recognized |
| Radius4 and8 promotion of those46 witnesses | 92 | Later coarse confirmation retained |

The existing rulebook's declared positive examples supply price/child templates.
The new generator constructs candles by interpolating their leaf anchors, with
explicit prefix/suffix bars for confirmation. Extension fixtures resolve the
selected motive children one further level. The parser receives only pivots
confirmed from those candles; declared node labels and child trees are comparison
targets and are not inserted into parser output. Exact matching checks family,
source indices, numeric prices and the complete recursive child topology.

These are symbolic prices in continuous invented H1 time beginning in2000. Each
candle has equal OHLC and a declared two-second publication delay. They do not
represent executable quotes, spreads, tick coverage or observed market sessions.
Parent family/position is supplied separately to check structural diagnostics; it
is not an automatically inferred D1/H4 context. No full strategy or account is run
on this corpus. The templates are representative examples, not an independent
validation of Elliott-wave theory or an exhaustive partition of possible patterns.
